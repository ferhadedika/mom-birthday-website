A Letter To You

Dear Mama,

Before I Go

So here is what I actually want to say, underneath all of this.

Happy birthday, Mama. I hope this year brings you the same peace and joy you've spent your whole life giving everyone else. I hope your health stays strong and your days stay full of small good things. I hope you get to rest more, worry less, and feel, even for one day, exactly how loved you are.

Here's to many more birthdays. Many more years of your laugh filling this house. Many more chances for me to tell you all of this again, because I plan to.

I love you today, and every ordinary day after this one.

Happy Birthday, Mama

With all my love,
